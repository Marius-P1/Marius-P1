### Hi, I'm Marius 👋

I'm an ML engineer at [We Spot Turtles!](https://wespotturtles.org), where I build recognition models that we use to help associations protect sea turtles. I'm in my final year at Epitech, after an exchange year at Korea University where I took courses in deep learning and computer vision.

I like working on projects that are useful to someone, because that makes them more meaningful to me. The one I'm most proud of is [Souviens Toi](https://github.com/Marius-P1/remember-me): it turns memories shared by the family into personalized stories, read aloud, to help Alzheimer's patients exercise their memory. I also love hackathons, and my team won first prize at SHIFT Nantes 2024 with an AI-run radio.

## 🔭 Currently

- Building the on-device turtle recognition model for the We Spot Turtles! app (TensorFlow, LiteRT, Flutter)
- Building EventDeer, our end-of-study project, with a team of five, as team lead, product owner and full stack developer
